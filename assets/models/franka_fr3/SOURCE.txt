Franka FR3 and Panda hand models from Google DeepMind MuJoCo Menagerie.
Original licenses retained in LICENSE-FR3 and LICENSE-PANDA.
https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/franka_fr3
https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/franka_emika_panda

QCP modifications: gravity compensation, torque actuators, 0.002 s timestep.
fr3.xml retains the earlier fixed-finger demonstration.
fr3-task.xml adds two actuated sliding fingers and a grip site, disables robot
collision shapes for the illustrative site-weld grasp, and retains FR3 meshes.
wine.xml, conveyor.xml, and safety.xml define original task setups, freely moving payloads,
contact geometry, and a switchable grip weld. safety.xml adds a shared workbench
and a static worker hand, peg feeder, insertion socket, and four illustrative clearance paths.
The conveyor pickup moves perpendicular to belt travel into a tray in front of the robot.
The wine delivery surface is circular. The restaurant/conveyor/assembly visuals and motion
plans were authored for this project and are explanatory examples.
